# privacy_policies: kept duplication

## Each product keeps its own legal text

The two policies share most of their sections, but each is a legal
text the DPO validates as written, for one product with its own data,
purposes and retention periods. A shared template would hide the text
behind locals: the DPO could no longer read one policy in one file,
and a change meant for one product could silently land in the other.

```parallel
views/api_entreprise/pages/_donnees_personnelles.html.erb
views/api_particulier/pages/_donnees_personnelles.html.erb
```
